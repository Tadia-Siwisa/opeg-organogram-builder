# Organogram Builder

**A self-serve, client-editable web app for building, branding, and exporting organizational charts — built to solve a real bottleneck: incomplete client data at project kickoff.**

🔗 [Live Project](https://tadia-siwisa.github.io/organogram-builder/)

---

## Overview

A client needed an organogram, but not all the organizational information was available at the time of the request. Rather than waiting on missing details — and risking a last-minute rush once they arrived — the project was redesigned around a different question: *what if the client could update the structure themselves, as information became available?*

What started as a single deliverable became a reusable, editable tool: a full organogram builder with autosave, custom branding, icon-based role tagging, and multi-format export — giving the client full autonomy over their own org chart, indefinitely, without needing to come back for revisions.

## Features

- **Editable org chart structure** — add roles and reporting lines dynamically
- **Custom branding** — upload a company logo/letterhead directly into the chart
- **Icon library** — 50+ role/division icons across categories (Energy & Industrial, Engineering & Construction, Logistics & Transport, Technology & Digital, Services & Support, Security & Safety, Finance & Business)
- **Autosave** — changes persist to the browser automatically
- **Export options** — Save as HTML, export to PDF (portrait or landscape), or print directly
- **Reset control** — full template reset with confirmation safeguard

## Design Principle

The core lesson behind this project: sometimes the best solution isn't working harder within an existing process — it's redesigning the process itself. Instead of treating an information gap as a blocker, the tool turns the client into an active participant in maintaining their own data, removing the dependency on back-and-forth revision requests entirely.
